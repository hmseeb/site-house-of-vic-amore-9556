Services

Come in with a question. Leave with a look that fits your life.

Most requested

Walk out with a cut that still looks right on a Tuesday morning

Cutting and styling built around your hair's real texture, density and growth pattern — not a photo that was never going to work. We talk through how much time you want to spend on your hair each day, then cut to that. You finish the appointment with the styling steps written for you, in order.

  • Consultation before a single section is cut
  • Dry cutting and shaping for curls, waves and coils
  • Blowouts, silk press and heat styling
  • Trims and shape maintenance between full appointments

Good to know

Appointments run long on purpose. There is no second client in the chair behind you, so a cut and style never gets rushed to the finish.

Color that grows out gracefully instead of leaving a line

Dimensional color, gloss and tone correction planned around how often you can realistically come back. If the colour you want needs more than one visit to reach safely, we will tell you that at the consultation rather than halfway through.

Bridal & bridal party · Event hair · Soft glam & natural makeup · Preview appointments available

The studio

A private chair on Lurting Avenue

House of Vic Amore is a small, private studio in the Pelham Gardens section of the Bronx, a few minutes from Pelham Parkway and easy to reach from Morris Park, Allerton, Eastchester and lower Westchester.

Because it is one chair and one client at a time, appointments are calm and unhurried. There is no overlapping schedule, no music fighting for attention and no stranger listening in while you talk about your hair. If you have had a bad experience somewhere loud and rushed, this is the opposite of that.

The studio is inside a residential building, so the exact apartment and entry details are sent to you with your appointment confirmation.
